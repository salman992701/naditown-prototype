Sime Darby Property - Township of Tomorrow 2026

Flood Resilience Operating Layer

NADITOWN is not another flood dashboard. It is the operating layer that turns township telemetry, field constraints and resident calls into governed, auditable response decisions.

People-first outcome

Prioritise residents by urgency, route reachability and verified shelter capacity. Keep a human commander in control of every high-impact action.

90-day pilot

Sector 2 flash-flood scenario in Bandar Elmina: validate SOS intake, route-aware dispatch, closure governance and audit exports with PKOB and township operations.

KPI gates

Median dispatch ETA, % requests matched within declared capacity, commute-time reduction, over-allocation incidents (target zero), audit-chain validity and resident time-to-request.

Integration path

Read-only feeds from CCTV/water sensors, traffic and facility systems; exportable SITREPs and audit packs; no rip-and-replace of existing command tools.

Commercial model

Per-township annual platform fee plus a paid pilot, with optional integration and resilience-reporting packages for developers, asset managers and local authorities.

Why now

Climate-driven flash flooding is becoming a recurring operating cost. A shared response layer protects people, reduces asset downtime and creates defensible ESG evidence.
